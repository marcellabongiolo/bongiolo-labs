# Bongiolo Labs

> Independent technology lab focused on artificial intelligence, software engineering, cloud systems, and experimental research.

Bongiolo Labs is a long-term technology project built around one principle: **learn by building**.

The lab is designed to grow alongside its engineering capabilities. Projects begin as experiments and can evolve into production-oriented software, research prototypes, developer tools, and cloud-native systems.

## Current focus

- Artificial Intelligence
- Software Engineering
- Cloud & Platform Engineering
- Data and temporal systems
- Distributed systems
- Technical research
- Interactive computing

## Flagship project

### AI 4D

AI 4D is the first experimental product of Bongiolo Labs.

The project explores how intelligent systems can represent and reason about information across **space and time**. It currently combines an interactive 4D visualization with an early temporal-prediction prototype.

→ [Explore AI 4D](https://github.com/marcellabongiolo/ai-4d)
