# Company

## About

Bongiolo Labs is an independent technology lab focused on building software and researching emerging technical ideas.

The lab brings together four complementary disciplines:

1. Artificial intelligence
2. Software engineering
3. Cloud and platform systems
4. Technical research

## Mission

Build useful, technically rigorous software while continuously exploring new approaches to intelligent and scalable systems.

## Vision

Develop a portfolio of technology products and research projects that demonstrate strong engineering, experimentation, and long-term technical thinking.

## Operating principles

### 01 — Build first

Ideas become valuable when they are implemented, tested, and documented.

### 02 — Learn continuously

Every project should create measurable technical learning.

### 03 — Engineer responsibly

Systems should prioritize reliability, security, maintainability, and clear documentation.

### 04 — Experiment honestly

Prototypes are clearly identified as prototypes. Research claims should be supported by evidence.

### 05 — Think long term

Projects are designed so that successful experiments can evolve into more complete systems.
